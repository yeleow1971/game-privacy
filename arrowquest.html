Privacy Policy — ArrowQuest

Android package com.yeleow.arrowquest · Last updated 30 August 2026

ArrowQuest does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Who we are

ArrowQuest is developed and published by Leow Yong Ean, Johor, Malaysia. Questions about this policy can be sent to yeleow@yahoo.com.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account system, no login, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, and no social features. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any usage statistics.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your phone so you can carry on where you left off:

  • Level progress, stars earned, and in-game coin balance
  • Player profile names that you type in yourself
  • Sound, music, and theme preferences

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

If you buy in-game coins, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play's billing system. Payment is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We never see or receive your card number, bank details, or billing address — only a confirmation from Google that a purchase completed, so the coins can be added to your game.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, storage, contacts, or any other sensitive feature of your device. The only permission declared is the one Google Play requires for in-app billing.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. The game contains no violence, no chat, no user-generated content, and no links out to other services.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The only external service involved is Google Play, which distributes the app and processes purchases.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting data — for example if advertising is added in a future version — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Leow Yong Ean · Johor, Malaysia · yeleow@yahoo.com

隐私政策 — ArrowQuest

Android 应用包名 com.yeleow.arrowquest · 最后更新 2026 年 8 月 30 日

ArrowQuest（箭头逃脱解谜游戏）不收集、不储存、不传输任何个人资料。

关于我们

ArrowQuest 由 Leow Yong Ean（马来西亚柔佛）开发和发布。有任何疑问请联系 yeleow@yahoo.com。

我们收集哪些资料

完全不收集。本游戏没有帐号系统、不需登录、没有数据分析、没有崩溃收集、没有广告 SDK、没有社交功能。我们不收集你的姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、通讯录、相册、设备识别码或任何使用数据。

储存在你手机上的资料

为了让你能接着上次继续玩，游戏会在你的手机本地保存：

  • 关卡进度、获得的星星、游戏内金币
  • 你自己输入的玩家档案名称
  • 音乐、音效、主题等设置

这些资料不会离开你的手机，不会传给我们或任何人，卸载游戏时会一并删除。

关于内购

如果你购买游戏内金币，整个交易由 Google Play 支付系统处理，适用 Google 隐私政策。我们看不到也不会收到你的卡号、银行资料或账单地址，只会收到 Google 发来的「购买完成」通知，用来把金币加到你的游戏里。

权限

本游戏不申请任何运行时权限，不会访问你的相机、麦克风、位置、储存空间、通讯录或其他敏感功能。唯一声明的权限是 Google Play 内购所必需的那一项。

儿童隐私

由于本游戏不收集任何人的资料，自然也不收集儿童的资料。游戏内没有暴力内容、没有聊天功能、没有用户生成内容，也没有跳转到其他服务的链接。

第三方

我们不与第三方共享资料，因为我们本来就不收集。唯一涉及的外部服务是 Google Play——负责分发应用和处理购买。

政策变更

如果将来版本开始收集资料（例如加入广告），我们会在该版本发布之前更新本页面，并修改顶部的日期。
